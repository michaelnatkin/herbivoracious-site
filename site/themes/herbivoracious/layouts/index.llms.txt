# Herbivoracious

> Vegetarian cooking for the food obsessed. Tested vegetarian and vegan recipes, technique guides and kitchen notes by Michael Natkin, author of the cookbook Herbivoracious: A Flavor Revolution (a James Beard Award finalist). Over 450 recipes; each recipe page has ingredients, steps, serving size, time and dietary notes, and many have answers to common questions.
