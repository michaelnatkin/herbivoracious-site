Indian-style cooking is a natural fit for vegetarians: lentils, chickpeas, cauliflower, potatoes and greens, all built up with whole spices and fresh aromatics. These are the Indian and Indian-style recipes on Herbivoracious, from a five-minute cabbage side dish to dals and curries that simmer while you do something else.

If you are new to this kind of cooking, start with the quick chana masala with mushrooms or the five-minute cabbage. Both are short, simple recipes.
