Korean cooking leans on fermented, spicy and savory flavors that translate well to vegetarian food. These recipes include kimchi fried rice, bibimbap, mandu dumplings, cold spicy noodles and the small side dishes known as banchan.

If you want to make your own kimchi, the full recipe covers fermenting napa cabbage at home, and the green apple version is a fast pickle in the same spirit.
