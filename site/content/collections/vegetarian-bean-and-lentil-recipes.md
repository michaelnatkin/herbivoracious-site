Dried beans and lentils are cheap, filling and endlessly adaptable. These recipes treat them as the main event: soups, stews, salads and pilafs from Ethiopia, Syria, Mexico, India and beyond.

Dried beans and lentils are also among the most affordable ingredients you can buy.
