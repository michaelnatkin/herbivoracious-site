Mushrooms bring the savory, meaty depth that vegetarian cooking sometimes needs. These recipes cover everyday cultivated mushrooms such as shiitake, king oyster and maitake, plus seasonal wild chanterelles, morels and porcini.

Browning them properly in the pan is where most of the flavor comes from.
