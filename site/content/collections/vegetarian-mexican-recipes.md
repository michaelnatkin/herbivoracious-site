Mexican food is full of dishes that never needed meat to begin with: beans, tortillas, chiles, salsas, eggs and masa. These are the Mexican and Latin-inspired recipes on Herbivoracious, from a weekend batch of tamales to a quick quesadilla.

The salsas and the red chile sauce are good places to start, because they improve almost everything else on the table.
