<p>This delicious salad is driven purely by its beautiful ingredients; all you have to do is take a little care to arrange them nicely.</p>
<p>Watermelon radishes are green on the outside, but when sliced you see that they are intensely red in the middle, looking much like tiny watermelons. If you have a mandoline, use it here: It is excellent for slicing them thinly and evenly.</p>
<p>Hydroponic watercress with the root ball intact has become available year-round at specialty markets. Look for bright, fresh leaves with no signs of wilt. The tart, peppery cress makes a great foil for the sweet figs and pomegranate arils.</p>

<div class="recipe-card">
<p><strong>Watermelon Radish and Watercress Salad</strong>
<br /><em>Gluten-free / Serves 4 / 10 minutes</em>
</p>
<ul>
<li>1 large watermelon radish, very thinly sliced</li>
<li>extra virgin olive oil</li>
<li>4 handfuls fresh watercress, rinsed and dried</li>
<li>1 lemon</li>
<li>4 ripe fresh figs, halved</li>
<li>¼ cup pomegranate arils</li>
<li>16 shavings of Parmgiano-Reggiano or other hard aged cheese</li>
<li>12 {{% product id="toasted-walnut-halves" %}}toasted walnut halves{{% /product %}}</li>
<li>{{% product id="maldon-salt-sea-salt-flakes-8-5-oz" %}}flaky sea salt{{% /product %}}</li>
</ul>
<ol>
<li>Arrange the slices of watermelon radish in a circle on 4 chlled plates. Drizzle with a little olive oil.</li>
<li>Toss the watercress with a bit of the olive oil and a squeeze of lemon juice. Place a fluffy handful on each plate. This is the critical step to making the salad look nice – aim to make a tall, high mound in the center, don’t let it spread out.</li>
<li>Add two fig halves, a tablespoon of pomegranate arils, 4 shavings of gouda, and 3 toasted walnut halves to each plate. Serve immediately.</li>
</ol>
