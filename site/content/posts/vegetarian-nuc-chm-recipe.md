<p>Nước chấm is the quintessential Vietnamese dipping sauce made of fish sauce seasoned with sugar, lime juice, garlic and chiles. I use a little bit of kombu and soy sauce to infuse that oceanic, umami flavor that would otherwise be missing.</p>
<p>Serve this with Banh xeo, or as an alternative to  the ginger-grapefruit sauce in Rice Vermicelli (Bun) with Ginger-Grapefruit Sauce.</p>

<div class="recipe-card">
<p><strong>Vegetarian Nước Chấm</strong>
<br /><em>Vegan; gluten-free option / Yields 1 cup / 10 minutes</em>
</p>
<ul>
<li>¾ cup water</li>
<li>2 tablespoons sugar</li>
<li>1 small piece dried {{% product id="dashi-kombu-dried-kelp-from-hokkaido-japan" %}}kombu seaweed{{% /product %}}</li>
<li>1-2 thinly sliced Thai bird or serrano chiles, or 2 teaspoons chile paste</li>
<li>1 tablespoon soy sauce (omit or check ingredients carefully if you need gluten-free)</li>
<li>6 tablespoons lime juice</li>
<li>2 cloves garlic, minced</li>
<li>about 1 teaspoon salt</li>
<li>¼ cup julienned carrots (optional)</li>
</ul>
<ol>
<li>Bring the water, sugar and kombu to a simmer. Turn off the heat and allow to stand for ten minutes. Remove the kombu.</li>
<li>Mix in the remaining ingredients. Taste and adjust seasoning to achieve a balance of savory, salty, sweet, spicy and sour. Serve immediately or keep in the refrigerator for a few days.</li>
</ol>
