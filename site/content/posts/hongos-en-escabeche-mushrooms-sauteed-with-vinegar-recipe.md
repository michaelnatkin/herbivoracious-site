<div class="faq"><h2>Frequently asked questions</h2>

<h3>What is hongos en escabeche?</h3>
<p>Mildly vinegared mushrooms sauteed in bite-sized chunks, so they have a browned, flavorful crust and a juicy interior. They are versatile and easy, and serve 4 as an appetizer or side dish.</p>

<h3>What mushrooms should I use?</h3>
<p>Small button or crimini mushrooms, cut into bite-sized chunks rather than thinly sliced, so they stay chewy.</p>

<h3>How can I change the flavor?</h3>
<p>Garnish with cilantro for a Mexican meal, or with parsley for a Spanish tapas spread. Switch to red wine vinegar, or a smaller amount of aged balsamic, and you are in Italy.</p>

<h3>What vinegar does the recipe use?</h3>
<p>Sherry vinegar, three tablespoons, added after the mushrooms and garlic are browned and cooked until absorbed.</p>
