<p>These little chickpea cakes are versatile; you can serve them as an appetizer, a side dish, or as the protein-packed star of the show. They are somewhat like a pan-fried falafel, but without a deep-fried crust, so we amp up the internal flavoring. Try them with the <a href="/2010/07/tomato-jam-with-rosemary-and-saffron-recipe/">Tomato Jam with Rosemary and Saffron</a> for a big flavor combination.</p>

<div class="recipe-card">
<p><strong>Chickpea Fritters</strong>
<br /><em>Makes 16 fritters / 30 minutes</em>
</p>
<ul>
<li>3 cups cooked chickpeas (or two 15 oz. cans, drained and rinsed)</li>
<li>1/2 cup green onions (white and light green parts only), very thinly sliced</li>
<li>5 cloves garlic, minced</li>
<li>1/4 teaspoon minced rosemary leaves</li>
<li>3/4 teaspoon Kosher salt</li>
<li>zest of 1 lemon</li>
<li>juice of 1/2 lemon</li>
<li>1 egg, lightly beaten</li>
<li>1/2 cup all-purpose flour</li>
<li>1/4 cup homemade breadcrumbs</li>
<li>1/4 cup {{% product id="toasted-sesame-seeds" %}}toasted sesame seeds{{% /product %}}</li>
<li>oil for shallow frying</li>
<li>{{% product id="maldon-salt-sea-salt-flakes-8-5-oz" %}}flaky sea salt{{% /product %}}</li>
</ul>
<ol>
<li>Combine all ingredients except the oil and flaky sea salt in a {{% product id="food-processor" %}}food processor{{% /product %}} and buzz until you have a fairly uniform mixture, but stop before it is completely pureed. We want some texture left. You should be able to form it into a ball that holds its shape. It should be neither crumbly nor a batter. Add liquid or breadcrumbs as necessary to find the right moisture level.</li>
<li>Heat a few tablespoons of oil in a large skillet, preferably {{% product id="lodge-12-inch-cast-iron-pre-seasoned-skillet" %}}cast-iron{{% /product %}}, over a medium-high flame. Take a golf-ball sized piece of dough, press it in your hands into a flattened 3-inch patty and place in the skillet. Repeat with as many as will fit comfortably. Fry on one side until golden brown, about 3 minutes, then flip and brown the other side. Remove to paper towels and finish with a little bit of sea salt.</li>
</ol>
</div>
